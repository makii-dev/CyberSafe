    Think you’ve been scammed?

    Act quickly and stay calm. It happens to many people.

    1. Stop replying. Do not click or pay anything else.
    2. Change the password of the affected account, then any account that shares it.
    3. Turn on 2FA and sign out of all other devices.
    4. Contact your bank or e-wallet provider to freeze cards or dispute payments.
    5. Tell your school IT team or a trusted adult, and keep screenshots as evidence.
    6. Report it to the authorities and to the platform.

    Where to report in the Philippines

    File a complaint with the PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group or the NBI Cybercrime Division, or use the CICC online form at cicc.gov.ph. Misuse of personal data can also go to the National Privacy Commission. Check each agency’s official website for current contact details.

    Glossary

    Phishing
    A fake message that tricks you into sharing logins, codes or money.
    Two-factor authentication (2FA)
    A second proof of identity, such as an app code, on top of your password.
    Malware
    Harmful software such as viruses, spyware or ransomware.
    VPN
    A service that encrypts your traffic and masks your IP address on untrusted networks.
    IP address and ISP
    The number that identifies your network online, and the company that connects you to the internet.
    Digital footprint
    The trail of data you leave through posts, searches, apps and logins.
    Data breach
    When attackers expose stored user data, such as emails and passwords.
    Entropy
    A measure, in bits, of how unpredictable a password is.
